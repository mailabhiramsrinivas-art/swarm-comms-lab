Ramjet Playground

Watch air get squeezed, heated and pushed out, then fly your own design. Read the explainer →

1 · InletThe cone (spike) meets the air first. Above Mach 1 it makes a shock wave, the slanted line, which starts slowing the air down.
2 · DiffuserThe duct gets wider, so the air slows down more. Slower air means higher pressure and temperature. This is the "ram" in ramjet.
3 · CombustorFuel burns in the squeezed air. The hot gas gets much bigger and wants to get out fast.
4 · NozzleThe hot gas squeezes through the throat and speeds out the back faster than it came in. That difference is thrust.
Pick a release height and speed, then press Launch.
