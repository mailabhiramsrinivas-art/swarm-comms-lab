Overview Live simulation

A drone swarm that keeps defending when its radio fails

Swarm Comms Lab is the software that lets a team of defensive drones work together. When the radio is good, they act as one team. When it is jammed or their leader is lost, they keep going: slower, but never stopped.

The problem: teamwork runs on radio

Threats arrive together

Several attackers come in at once. Each defending drone must know which one is its job, so two don't chase the same target while another slips through.

Splitting the work needs radio

To divide the targets, drones share what they see and who is taking what, over radio links between them.

Radio is the weak point

Links get jammed, fade with distance, or the station giving orders is destroyed. A swarm that freezes without its radio is no defence at all.

Our answer: three modes, picked automatically

Every drone runs the same program. Many times a second it checks what it can still hear, then uses the best of three modes. Nobody has to switch it.

Good radioNo radio
Blue

Follow the team plan

A leader works out the best job for every drone and sends the plan five times a second.

Amber

Agree with neighbours

If the plan stops arriving, drones that can still hear each other share out the threats. The best-placed drone takes each one.

Red

Act alone

If a drone hears nobody, it goes after the most urgent threat its own camera can see.

If the leader is destroyed, another drone takes over by itself in about one second. Try it in the live demo.
