What's next

In October 2026 the project went from a first simulator to a trained decision AI. Here is what has been built, and the steps that turn it into one complete system.

Built so far

  1. 12 Oct 2026Communication simulator

    The three modes, automatic leader handover and five test situations.

  2. 13 Oct 20263D game viewer

    Recorded formation games played back in 3D, with video export for presentations.

  3. 20 Oct 2026Decision AI in the browser

    The trained AI running on any computer, with a builder to test it on your own formations.

Next steps

  1. Step 1Tell threats apart

    Drones work out for themselves which sighting belongs to which threat, instead of being told.

  2. Step 2Plug in the trained AI

    Replace the hand-written targeting rules with a trained model.

  3. Step 3Use less radio

    Smarter message passing, so radio traffic stays manageable as the swarm grows.

  4. Step 4Record everything

    Log every message and decision, to train the next generation of models.

  5. Step 5Let drones learn what to say

    Compare learned messaging against today's hand-designed messages.

  6. Step 63D world, realistic flight

    Move from a flat map to full 3D with realistic flight physics.

See it for yourself

Everything on this site runs in your browser. Break the radio in the live demo, or build an enemy formation and watch the AI take it apart.
