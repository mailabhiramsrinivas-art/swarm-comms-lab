    How to read it

    D must eliminate every O player within 2 ticks. In each tick everyone moves, the formation is read, and each D player may hit one exposed O player inside its sphere. Shielded players only become hittable once the players around them are gone, so a formation is peeled from the outside in.

    Exposed O player. Can be hit.
    Shielded O player. Inside the formation with every link around it supported, so it cannot be hit.
    Eliminated. Fades out.
    D player. Its translucent sphere has radius r: the range of its hit.
    Supported link (shorter than 2r) in the formation's triangulation.
    Unsupported link (2r or longer).
    Nerve. Two D players who could hit the same O player, so they must agree who takes it.
    Hit. White when it eliminates, amber when wasted (someone else hit that player), red when blocked (the target was shielded).

    The phases of a tick

    1. Move. Everyone moves at once. O flees the nearest D player while keeping its formation together.
    2. Attack. Each D player may hit one exposed O player within its sphere. All hits land at the same moment.
    3. Plan. The survivors are read again. This is the picture D plans the next tick from.

    The readout shows the dimension of the formation's shape: 3 with four or more survivors, then a triangle (2), an edge (1) and a single point (0).
